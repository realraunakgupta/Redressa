## Section 1: Flight Cancellation by Airline

### 1.1 Notice Period and Compensation
If the airline cancels a flight, the following compensation is applicable based on the notice period provided to the passenger:
- If notified less than 24 hours before departure: Full refund of ticket cost OR alternate flight. Additionally, compensation of INR 5,000 to INR 10,000 depending on the block time of the flight.
- If notified between 24 hours and 2 weeks before departure: Full refund OR alternate flight. Compensation of INR 2,500 to INR 5,000.
- If notified more than 2 weeks before departure: Full refund OR alternate flight. No additional compensation required.

### 1.2 Refund Timeline
Airlines must process refunds within 15 days of the cancellation for tickets purchased directly from the airline. For tickets purchased through agents or third parties, the airline must initiate the refund within 15 days, and the agent must pass it to the passenger within 30 days.

### 1.3 Alternate Flight Obligation
If the passenger opts for an alternate flight, the airline must provide the next available flight at no additional cost. If the next available flight is on a different date, the airline must provide meal vouchers and hotel accommodation if the wait exceeds 6 hours.

## Section 2: Flight Delay

### 2.1 Delay Thresholds and Entitlements
When a flight is delayed at the departure airport:
- Delay of 2 hours or more but less than 6 hours: Meals and refreshments must be provided.
- Delay of 6 hours or more: Meals, refreshments, and hotel accommodation (if the delay extends overnight) must be provided. The passenger may also opt for a full refund.
- If the airline fails to inform the passenger of a delay of more than 6 hours at least 24 hours before departure, compensation of INR 5,000 to INR 10,000 is applicable.

### 2.2 Tarmac Delay
If passengers are kept on the aircraft on the tarmac for more than 2 hours, the airline must provide drinking water, access to lavatories, and medical assistance if required. After 3 hours on the tarmac, passengers must be given the option to deplane.

## Section 3: Denied Boarding (Overbooking)

### 3.1 Compensation for Denied Boarding
If a passenger with a confirmed ticket is denied boarding due to overbooking:
- The airline must first seek volunteers who are willing to give up their seats in exchange for benefits.
- If no volunteers are found, involuntary denied boarding compensation applies:
  - For flights with block time up to 1 hour: INR 10,000 or 200% of the basic fare plus fuel surcharge, whichever is less.
  - For flights with block time of 1 to 2 hours: INR 10,000 or 200% of the basic fare plus fuel surcharge, whichever is less.
  - For flights with block time of more than 2 hours: INR 20,000 or 400% of the basic fare plus fuel surcharge, whichever is less.
- Additionally, the airline must arrange an alternate flight or provide a full refund.

## Section 4: Baggage

### 4.1 Lost Baggage
If checked baggage is lost by the airline, the passenger is entitled to compensation up to INR 20,000 for domestic flights. The airline must register a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) at the airport. If the baggage is not found within 21 days of filing the PIR, it is deemed lost and compensation must be paid within 30 days.

### 4.2 Damaged Baggage
If checked baggage is received in a damaged condition, the passenger must report it at the airport baggage counter before leaving. Compensation for damage is subject to proof of contents and their value, up to the applicable liability limit.

## Section 5: Complaint and Escalation

### 5.1 Airline Grievance Cell
Passengers should first file a complaint directly with the airline's grievance cell. The airline must acknowledge the complaint within 7 days and resolve it within 30 days.

### 5.2 DGCA Escalation
If the airline does not resolve the complaint within 30 days, or the resolution is unsatisfactory, the passenger may escalate the complaint to the DGCA Air Passenger Grievance Redressal Authority via the AirSewa portal (https://airsewa.gov.in) or by emailing passenger-grievance@dgca.nic.in.

### 5.3 Consumer Forum
Passengers also retain the right to approach the Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
