## Section 1: Return Window

### 1.1 Standard Return Window
Flipkart's return window varies by product category:
- Electronics (mobile phones, laptops, tablets): 7 days from delivery.
- Appliances (large and small): 7 days from delivery.
- Fashion (clothing, footwear, accessories): 10 to 30 days depending on the seller.
- Furniture: 10 days from delivery.
- Books, media, and general items: 10 days from delivery.

### 1.2 No-Return Products
Some products are not eligible for return:
- Innerwear, lingerie, socks, and swimwear.
- Customized or personalized products.
- Products with tampered or missing serial numbers.
- Software, digital content, and gift cards after activation.

## Section 2: Damaged Item Returns

### 2.1 Eligibility
If a product is received in a damaged condition (physical damage during transit), the customer must:
- Report the issue within the applicable return window.
- Provide photographic evidence of the damage via the return request.
- Keep the original packaging until the return is processed.

### 2.2 How to Request Return
1. Go to "My Orders" on Flipkart app or website.
2. Select the order and click "Return."
3. Choose reason: "Product was damaged upon delivery."
4. Upload photos showing the damage.
5. Select refund or replacement.

### 2.3 Pickup and Refund
- Flipkart arranges a pickup for the damaged item within 2 to 5 business days.
- Refund is processed within 7 to 10 business days after the item is picked up and verified.
- Refund goes to the original payment method. For COD (Cash on Delivery) orders, refund is issued to bank account or Flipkart wallet.

## Section 3: Defective Item Returns

### 3.1 Eligibility
If a product is defective (not functioning as intended or has a manufacturing defect):
- The defect must be reported within the return window.
- For products under manufacturer warranty, Flipkart may direct the customer to the brand's authorized service centre after the return window expires.

### 3.2 Process
- Same return request process as damaged items (Section 2.2).
- Choose reason: "Product is defective / not working."
- Flipkart may request a video demonstration of the defect for electronics.

### 3.3 Warranty vs. Return
- Within return window: Full refund or replacement via Flipkart.
- After return window but within warranty: Customer must contact the brand's authorized service centre. Flipkart is not liable for post-window warranty claims.

## Section 4: Wrong Item Received

### 4.1 Wrong Product
If the customer receives a product different from what was ordered:
- Report within the return window.
- Provide photos of the received item and packaging.
- Flipkart arranges pickup and provides full refund or replacement.

### 4.2 Missing Items from Order
If an item is missing from a multi-item order:
- Report within 48 hours of delivery.
- Flipkart investigates and provides refund or ships the missing item.

## Section 5: Refund Modes and Timelines

### 5.1 Refund Timelines
After the returned item is picked up and verified:
- Flipkart wallet / gift card: Instant to 24 hours.
- UPI / net banking: 5 to 7 business days.
- Credit/debit card: 7 to 10 business days.
- NEFT (for COD orders): 7 to 10 business days.
- Phone credits (for Flipkart Phone Exchange): 7 to 10 business days.

### 5.2 Partial Refunds
Flipkart may issue a partial refund if:
- The returned item is not in its original condition.
- Accessories, free gifts, or components are missing.
- The product shows signs of use beyond basic inspection.

## Section 6: Escalation

### 6.1 Flipkart Customer Support
- In-app chat: Flipkart app -> Help Centre -> Chat with us.
- Email: cs@flipkart.com (response within 24 to 48 hours).
- Call: 1800-202-9898 (toll-free, available 24/7).

### 6.2 Escalation Path
If the issue is not resolved through customer support:
- Grievance Officer: grievance.officer@flipkart.com
- Consumer Protection: File a complaint on the National Consumer Helpline (consumerhelpline.gov.in) or call 1800-11-4000.
- Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum: File a case under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

### 6.3 Seller-Specific Issues
For products sold by third-party sellers on Flipkart:
- Flipkart acts as a marketplace intermediary.
- The seller is responsible for product quality and warranty.
- Flipkart facilitates the return/refund process but the seller bears the cost.
