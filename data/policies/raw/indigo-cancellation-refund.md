## Section 1: Cancellation by Passenger

### 1.1 Cancellation Fee
Passengers may cancel their booking and request a refund, subject to the following cancellation charges:
- Cancellation made more than 72 hours before departure: INR 3,500 per passenger per sector cancellation fee.
- Cancellation made between 72 hours and 4 hours before departure: INR 3,500 per passenger per sector cancellation fee.
- Cancellation made less than 4 hours before departure or no-show: The fare is non-refundable for standard bookings.
- For flexi fares: Lower cancellation fees may apply. Check fare rules at time of booking.

### 1.2 Refund Processing
Refunds are processed to the original mode of payment. Processing time:
- Credit card: 7 to 14 business days.
- Debit card or net banking: 5 to 7 business days.
- IndiGo wallet / 6E Rewards: Within 24 hours.
- Cash payment at airport counter: Refund via NEFT, 7 to 10 business days.

### 1.3 How to Cancel
Cancellation can be done through:
- IndiGo website (goindigo.in) under "Manage Booking"
- IndiGo mobile app
- IndiGo call centre: 0124-6173838
- Airport counter (subject to higher fees)

## Section 2: Cancellation by IndiGo

### 2.1 Airline-Initiated Cancellation
When IndiGo cancels a flight, passengers are entitled to:
- Full refund of the ticket amount (including all taxes and fees), processed within 15 days.
- OR rebooking on the next available IndiGo flight at no extra cost.
- If the wait for the next flight exceeds 6 hours, IndiGo will provide meal vouchers.
- If the wait extends overnight, IndiGo will arrange hotel accommodation.

### 2.2 Compensation (DGCA-mandated)
In addition to refund or rebooking, compensation as per DGCA passenger charter rules applies based on the notice period provided. IndiGo follows the DGCA-mandated compensation rates for flight cancellation with less than 2 weeks' notice.

## Section 3: Refund for Flight Delays

### 3.1 Delay of More Than 6 Hours
If a flight is delayed by more than 6 hours from the scheduled departure, passengers may opt for:
- Full refund.
- Rebooking on the next available flight.
IndiGo provides meals and refreshments for delays exceeding 2 hours at the departure airport.

## Section 4: Special Cases

### 4.1 Medical Emergency Cancellation
In case of medical emergency (passenger or immediate family), IndiGo may waive the cancellation fee upon submission of valid medical documentation. The request must be made before the flight departure time.

### 4.2 Schedule Change by IndiGo
If IndiGo changes the flight schedule by more than 2 hours, the passenger is entitled to a full refund or free rebooking.

## Section 5: Contact and Escalation
