## Return Policy Overview

Myntra's Returns and Exchange Policy offers you the option to return or exchange items purchased on Myntra within 14 days of receipt (up to 30 days for certain premium categories). We only ask that you don't use the product and preserve its original condition, tags, and packaging. You are welcome to try on a product but please take adequate measure to preserve its condition. 

## Defective or Damaged Products

If you receive a defective or damaged product, or if the product does not match the description on the platform, you must raise a complaint within 48 hours of delivery. 
You are required to attach clear photographs of the defective item and the packaging in which it was delivered. Once verified, Myntra will initiate a reverse pickup and process a replacement or full refund, including shipping charges.

## Missing Items or Wrong Deliveries

If you find that an item is missing from your package or you have received the wrong item, please report the issue to Myntra Customer Support within 48 hours of delivery. 
Myntra may require you to provide evidence such as the unbroken seal (if applicable) or photos of the received items. Upon successful verification, the correct product will be shipped or a refund will be issued.

## Refund Process and Timelines

Once your returned item is received at our warehouse and passes the quality check, your refund will be initiated within 48 hours.
The refund amount is expected to reflect in your source account within:
- Credit Card / Debit Card: 5-7 Business Days
- Net Banking / UPI: 2-5 Business Days
- Myntra Credit / Wallet: 24 Hours

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, users must provide their bank account details via the app to receive an NEFT transfer. The NEFT transfer usually takes 3-5 business days.

## Non-Returnable Items

Certain categories of products cannot be returned or exchanged due to hygiene and safety reasons. These include:
- Innerwear, lingerie, swimwear, and sleepwear
- Grooming products, perfumes, and deodorants
- Fragile items and jewelry
- Any free gifts or promotional items sent along with the package

If a non-returnable item is defective or damaged upon delivery, the customer must raise the issue within 24 hours of receiving the package.

## Grievance Redressal and Escalation

Consumers can raise initial complaints via the "Help Center" or "Contact Us" feature within the Myntra App. 
If the complaint is not resolved within 7 business days, the Consumer can escalate the issue to the Grievance Officer at grievance.officer@myntra.com. The Grievance Officer is obligated to acknowledge the complaint within 48 hours and resolve the dispute within 30 days as per the E-Commerce Rules.
